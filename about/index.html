About North-Valley Islamic Centre

A not-for-profit charitable organization incorporated in the province of Ontario, devoted to serving the communities living in Brampton and surrounding areas.

Who Are We?

Serving Brampton with faith

North-Valley Islamic Centre (NICE) is a not-for-profit charitable organization which has been incorporated in the province of Ontario. NICE is an initiative which strives to develop a dynamic community where families should be able to fulfill all of their divine and social needs with comfort and ease. In this regard, our ultimate goal is to establish a unique environment for all of us.

NICE shall be a charitable, non-profit, non-political, Islamic religious organization, and shall be devoted to the services of the communities living in Brampton and surrounding areas. NICE will carry out its objectives and all activities in accordance with Al-Qur'an and the Traditions of Prophet Muhammad (pbuh).

Mission & Vision

A center of excellence for our community

NICE strives to become a center of excellence for community support and socio-cultural development for women, youth and adults.

To establish, operate and maintain NICE for providing community services for the Muslims living in Brampton and the region, NICE will have the following, based on the community's needs:

  • A mosque
  • An Islamic school
  • Library
  • Adult education
  • Employment training centre
  • Fitness centre
  • Funeral services
  • Support for elderly people

Main Objectives

1

To preach and advance the teachings of the Islamic faith and the religious tenets, doctrines, observances and culture associated with that faith.

2

To establish, maintain and support a house of worship (mosque) with services conducted in accordance with the tenets and doctrines of the Islamic faith.

3

To support and maintain missions and missionaries in order to propagate the Islamic faith.

4

To establish and maintain a religious school of instruction for children, youths and adults.
