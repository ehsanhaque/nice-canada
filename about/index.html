About North-Valley Islamic Centre

A not-for-profit charitable organization incorporated in the province of Ontario, dedicated to serving the Muslim community of Brampton and beyond.

Our Mission

A center of excellence for our community

North-Valley Islamic Centre (NICE) is a not-for-profit charitable organization which has been incorporated in the province of Ontario. We seek to establish a center of excellence that supports the socio-cultural development of women, youth, and adults in our community.

Our goal is to develop a dynamic community where families are able to fulfill all of their divine and social needs — from daily worship and Islamic education to youth mentorship, family support, and outreach to those in need.

NICE follows the school of thought of Ahlus Sunnah Wal-Jama'ah, and follows the Hilal Council of Canada for moon-sighting methodology in determining Islamic dates.

Our Objectives

What we're working to build

1

Advance the teachings of the Islamic faith and associated religious practices.

2

Establish and maintain a mosque with services aligned to Islamic doctrine.

3

Support missionary efforts promoting the Islamic faith.

4

Operate a religious school for children, youths, and adults.

Looking Ahead

Services we're building toward

As our community grows, NICE is working to expand into a full-service centre offering:

  • A dedicated mosque and prayer facility
  • Full-time Islamic school
  • Community library
  • Adult education & employment training
  • Fitness facilities
  • Elderly support services
